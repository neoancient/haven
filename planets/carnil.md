      The fourth planet in the Yrth system, Carnil was the first planet outside Yrth to have a permanent settlement, but

      to make it habitable by altering the internal geology to generate the necessary heat. Carnil's low gravity lends itself
      to certain types of scientific research, low gravity sports, and retirement communities.
