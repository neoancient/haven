Skills represents a character's competencies. Each skill has a ranking that indicates how good a character is at actions
using that skill. Skills not appearing on the character sheet are understood to have a level of Mediocre (+0). Most people
are Average (+1) or Fair (+2) at skills involved in their professions, with anything Good (+3) or higher being a mark
of some expertise. Player characters are exceptional, and have multiple skills at these higher levels.

Use of a skill falls into one of five categories:

- Overcome
- Create an advantage
- Attack
- Defend
- Block

An **Overcome** action is an attempt to deal with an obstacle or obstruction or resolve an undesired situation.

A **Create an Advantage** action attempts to alter the situation in a way that gives the character or their allies an
advantage. Success creates a situational aspect that can later be invoked to cash in on the advantage.

An **Attack** action is an attempt to defeat another character. It does not necessarily involve physical harm. It can also include
attempts to intimidate or bluff your way past someone.

A **Defend** action is an attempt to fend off another character's **Attack** action.

For more information on skills and actions, see [Playing the Game]({{ site.baseurl }}/playing_the_game.html)

# Skill Descriptions with Examples
## Athletics
The **Athletics** skill involves physical activities requiring speed or agility. Feats of strength are covered by **Physique**.
It covers movement outside a vehicle, including running, jumping, climbing, and swimming.
 - **Overcome:**
   - Climb a rope
   - Jump a chasm
   - Move to another zone when there is an obstacle in the way
   - Move additional zones
   - Racing on foot, swimming, or bicycles.
 - **Advantage:**
   - Seize the high ground
   - Flank an opponent
   - Confound opponent with acrobatic maneuvers
   - Get out of an opponent's reach
 - **Defense:**
   - Defend against physical attack, either close-quarters or ranged.
   - Defend against opponent trying to move past you.

## Burglary
The **Burglary** skill is used for stealing things, getting into restricted places, and dealing with security systems.
It can also be used for preventing others doing the same to you.
- **Overcome:**
  - Override the lock on a door
  - Disable a trap
  - Bypass security system
  - Pick a pocket
  - Cover tracks
  - Conceal an item so it escapes notice
- **Advantage:**
  - Case a location
  - Study security measures
  - Discover security vulnerabilities
  - Analyze someone else's burglary effort and discover aspects related to their work.
- **Defend:**
  - Enact countermeasures against someone else's attempt to breach security

## Contacts
The **Contacts** skill is about knowing people who know things or can get things done.
- **Overcome:**
  - Find a person or object
- **Advantage:** Contacts can be used to find the right person to talk to. The aspect created is often used to create
a story detail. It can also be used to plant a rumor.
  - Find the right person to talk to
  - Plant a rumor
  - Create a story detail, such as _A Guy Who Handles This Kind of Merchandise_
- **Defend:**
  - Someone using their network to find you
  - Someone spreading false (or true) information
  - Prevent someone from vanishing from the reach of your network

## Culture
The **Culture** skill takes the place of another skill in situations that reflect a character's background. Each
character can select three situations in which the Culture skill can be used in place of the skill that would normally
be used. For more information, see [Species and Culture]({{ site.baseurl }}/species_and_culture.html).

## Deceive
The **Deceive** skill is about lying and misdirection. Actions against a minor NPC can be a simple overcome action, but
an action against a named NPC is a competition, opposed by **Empathy**.
- **Overcome:**
  - Bluff your way past someone
  - Get someone to believe a lie
  - Disguise yourself
- **Advantage:**
  - Create a distraction
  - Create a cover story
  - Create a false impression
  - Trick someone into revealing useful information
  - Forge documents
- **Defend:**
  - Defend against **Gather Information**
  - Defend against attempts to learn something using **Empathy**

## Discipline
The **Discipline** skill is about self-control.
- **Overcome:**
    - Stay awake
    - Navigate past hazards such as a minefield or a debris field (in place of **Athletics** or **Pilot**)
    - Avoid disrupting technology with magical effects
    - Avoid setting off an arcane detector
- **Advantage:** 
  - Prepare mentally or psychologically for a demanding task
- **Defend:**
    - Defend against **Provoke**
    - Defend against **Empathy** or **Rapport** when used to try to reveal a secret
    - Defend against attempts to detect magic use with **Lore**

## Empathy
The **Empathy** skill is about noticing mood bearing.
- **Overcome**:
  - Notice a change in mood
- **Advantage:**
  - Discover or create an aspect on another character
- **Defend:**
  - Defend against **Deceive**
  - Defend against attempts to create social advantages with **Rapport**
**Special:** Empathy is used to begin the healing process for mental consequences.

## Fight
The **Fight** skill is about attempts to harm or subdue an opponent within the same zone. It covers both armed and
unarmed combat. For ranged combat, use **Shoot**.
- **Overcome:** 
  - Sport fighting, such as wrestling or fencing (as a competition)
  - Knowledge about melee weapons or fighting styles
  - Assess a melee weapon
- **Advantage:**
  - Disarm an opponent
  - Dirty tricks, such as kicking sand in the opponent's face
  - Judge a tactical situation
  - Assess opponent's fighting style
- **Attack:**
  - Make a physical attack against an opponent in the same zone
- **Defend:**
  - Defend against **Fight**

## Investigate
The **Investigate** skill is about gathering information through effort. For situational awareness and quick observations,
use **Notice**. For gathering information through social networks, use **Contacts**. For gathering arcane knowledge,
use **Lore**.
- **Overcome**:
  - Research
  - Find a passage in a book
  - Eavesdrop on a conversation
  - Find clues at a crime scene
- **Advantage:**
  - Discover a detail about something or someone, given enough time (very versatile)

## Lore
The **Lore** skill is about magical knowledge and awareness.
- **Overcome**:
  - Apply knowledge of magic
  - Recognize the presence of active magic
  - Detect residual magical energy
  - Research a magic ritual
- **Advantage:**
  - Use knowledge of magic to add an aspect or story detail

## Mechanics
The **Mechanics** skill is about working with machinery — usually to fix it, but sometimes to build or damage it.
- **Overcome:**
  - Repair an item
  - Build a mechanical device
  - Upgrade a mechanical device
  - Sabotage a mechanical device
- **Advantage:**
  - Study a device and discover an aspect
  - Create a makeshift device that becomes a scene aspect
- **Attack:** 
  - Build a booby trap
  - Arm a mine

## Notice
The **Notice** skill is about surface observations and situational awareness of mundane things. For gathering information
through effort, use **Investigate**.
  - Become aware of something in the scene
- **Advantage:**
  - Place a story detail in the scene, such as an escape route or a precariously balanced statue
- **Defend:**
  - Defend against **Stealth**

## Physique
The **Physique** skill is about physical strength and endurance. For speed and agility, use **Athletics**.
- **Overcome:**
  - Open a stuck door
  - Bend prison bars
  - Lift a boulder
  - Contest of strength, such as arm wrestling 
- **Advantage:**
  - Restrain a target
  - Discover a physical impairment during a physical conflict (e.g. _Bum Leg_)
- **Defend:**
  - Oppose someone else's movement
**Special:** A Physique of Average (+1) or Fair (+2) gives an additional physical stress box. A Physique of Good (+3)
or Great (+4) gives two additional physical stress boxes. A Physique of Superb (+5) or higher gies two additional
physical stress boxes and an extra mild consequence slot, which can only be used for physical damage.

## Pilot
The **Pilot** skill is about controlling a vehicle, whether a ground vehicle, an atmospheric craft, or a starship.
- **Overcome:**
  - Outrun an opponent
  - Fly/drive through a narrow canyon/alley
  - Outrun an opponent (as a contest)
  - Land on a hangar deck at full throttle
- **Advantage:**
  - Maneuver onto someone's tail
  - Find a shortcut
  - Find a less dangerous route
  - Discover an aspect of another vehicle
- **Attack:**
  - Ram another vehicle. The attacking vehicle takes the same amount of stress as the target.
- **Defend:**
  - Defend against attacks made against the vehicle

## Provoke
The **Provoke** skill is used to elicit a negative response from someone else. For attempts at a positive respone, use
**Rapport**. Provoke is about being a jerk. Against PCs or named NPCs, it is defended by **Discipline**.
- **Overcome:**
  - Intimidate for information
  - Incite to violence
  - Scare into fleeing
- **Advantage:**
  - Create temporary aspect representing an emotional state
- **Attack:**
  - Provoke can be used to make mental attacks.

## Rapport
The **Rapport** skill is used to elicit a positive response from someone else. For attempts at a negative response, use
**Provoke**. Rapport is about being liked and trusted. Against PCs or names NPCs, it is defended by **Discipline**.
- **Overcome:**
  - Charm your way past the guard or the bouncer
  - Deescalate an angry opponent
- **Advantage:**
  - Inspire allies
  - Gain someone's confidence
  - Convince someone to be helpful
- **Defend:**
  - Attempts to damage your reputation
  - Make you look bad in front of others

## Resources
The **Resources** skill is a measure of a character's wealth. It is an abstraction that allows handling wealth without
the bookkeeping.
- **Overcome:**
  - Bribe your way past the guard
  - Avoid red tape
  - Assess an object's value
- **Advantage:**
  - Impress people who are impressed by wealth
  - Buy friends
  - Acquire the loyalty of someone on the inside

## Science
The **Science** skill represents knowledge and education. It is science in the broad sense of the word, dealing with
non-magical knowledge in general. For magical knowledge, use **Lore**.
- **Overcome:**
  - Apply knowledge of physical science, social science, medicine, history _inter alia_ to get past an obstacle.
- **Advantage:**
  - Use an area of knowledge to apply a story detail
**Special:** Science is used to begin the healing process for physical consequences.

## Shoot
The **Shoot** skill is used to make attacks from up to two zones away. It includes attacks from thrown weapons, sling, bows,
firearms, energy weapons, etc. For close-quarters combat, use **Fight**.
- **Overcome:**
  - Demonstrate shooting proficiency, such as with a contest
  - Assess a ranged weapon
- **Advantage:**
  - Aim
  - Suppressing fire
  - Trick shot
  - Create an aspect for an opponent's weapon based on weapon knowledge
- **Attack:**
  - Make a physical attack

## Stealth
The **Stealth** skill is about avoiding detection.
- **Overcome:**
  - Sneak past security
  - Hide from a pursuer
  - Cover tracks
- **Advantage:**
  - Set an ambush
- **Defend:**
  - Defend against **Notice**

## Will
The **Will** skill is about mental strength. It is also used for creating a magical effect. It can be used for dealing
with a mental task that is only a matter of time. If it takes more than mental brute force, use **Science**.
- **Overcome:**
  - Solve a puzzle requiring mental effort
  - Decipher a code
  - Play chess (contest)
  - Use magic
- **Advantage:**
  - Place aspects on yourself involving resistance to mental stress
  - Suppress magic in the area
- **Attack:**
  - Magical attacks can be either physical or mental
- **Defend:**
  - Defend against mental attacks from magical sources. Physical attacks normally use **Athletics** for defense.

**Special:** A Will of Average (+1) or Fair (+2) gives an additional mental stress box. A Will of Good (+3)
or Great (+4) gives two additional mental stress boxes. A Will of Superb (+5) or higher gies two additional
mental stress boxes and an extra mild consequence slot, which can only be used for mental damage.
