# Extras

Extras are a catch-all category for abilities or possessions under the character's control that requires special
treatment in the rules. Like stunts, characters must spend refresh to acquire extras. One example of an extra is
magic, which is treated in a separate chapter. Other extras include supernatural powers, cybernetic enhancements,
special gear, vehicles and starships, locations, and organizations.

Some extras need a permission, which is a prerequisite a character needs to have the extra. A lot of gear does not
need any permission; basically anyone could have an enchanted sword. But a holy weapon attached to a religious order
would probably require some attachment to that order, usually involving at least an aspect, to use its abilities.
Magic likewise requires either an aspect connecting the practitioner to a school of magic or a cultural stunt to unlock
access to a realm. Supernatural strength would be a natural part of being a troll or a giant, but humans would need
cybernetic enhancements (or pharmaceutical, common with Yrth Marines).

## Enhanced Abilities
Enhanced abilities are those that go beyond those of the typical player species. They may be natural to other species,
or supernatural, or technological enhancements.

### Breath Weapon
- Minor Breath Weapon [2]
  - Can make a ranged elemental attack using the **Shoot** skill.
  - Attack has Weapon:2 quality
- Moderate Breath Weapon [4]
  As above, but the attack has the Weapon:4 quality.
- Major Breath Weapon [6]
  As above, but the attack has the Weapon:6 quality.

### Leaping
- Inhuman Leaping [2]
  - +2 to Athletics for vertical jumps or long leaps.
  - Can safely drop from up to 3 stories without rolling.
- Supernatural Leaping [4]
  - +4 to Athletics for vertical jumps or long leaps.
  - Can clear small buildings or cover miles in a single bound.
- Mythic Leaping [6]
  - Movement ignores most terrain obstacles; can leap kilometers in a single bound.

### Recovery/Regeneration
Entities with fast recovery need to have a type of attack that they recover from normally (e.g. fire, cold iron, silver).
- Inhuman Recovery [2]
    - Can recover from physical harm that would otherwise be permanent damage.
    - Out of conflict, can recover from physical consequences as if they were one level lower in severity. A mild consequence is removed by the beginning of the next scene.
    - Can take an action to remove one mild physical consequence once per scene.
- Supernatural Recovery [4]
    - Can recover from physical harm that would otherwise be permanent damage.
    - Out of conflict, can recover from physical consequences as if they were two levels lower in severity. A mild or moderate consequence is removed by the beginning of the next scene.
    - Can take an action to remove one mild physical consequence twice per scene.
- Mythic Recovery [6]
    - Can recover from physical harm that would otherwise be permanent damage.
    - Out of conflict, can recover from all physical consequences.
    - Can take an action to remove one mild physical consequence three times per scene.

### Speed
- Inhuman Speed [2]
  - **Notice** is at +4 for determining turn order in physical conflict.
  - All athletics checks are made at +1 excepting sprinting, which is +2.
  - Add +2 to **Stealth** checks involving movement.
- Supernatural Speed [4]
  - Always go before anyone without at least supernatural speed in a physical conflict.
  - All athletics checks are made at +2 except sprinting, which is +4.
  - Reduce difficulty by 4 when moving using **Stealth**.
  - Add +4 to **Stealth** checks involving movement.
- Mythic Speed [6]
    - Always go before anyone without at least mythic speed in a physical conflict.
    - All athletics checks are made at +3 except sprinting, which is +6.
    - Reduce difficulty by 4 when moving using **Stealth**.
    - Add +6 to **Stealth** checks involving movement.

### Senses
- Inhuman Senses [2]
   - +2 to Notice checks involving your enhanced sense(s).
   - Can detect things outside normal human ranges (e.g., infrared vision, ultrasound hearing).
   - Once per scene, ask the GM one free question about your surroundings as if using Investigate without rolling.
- Supernatural Senses [4]
  - +4 to Notice checks involving your enhanced sense(s).
  - Ignore up to 2 points of difficulty from environmental penalties (fog, darkness, loud noise).
  - You always act before anyone without supernatural senses in contests of perception.
- Mythic Senses [6]
  - Can perceive across vast distances, through some solid barriers, or directly sense magic/psionics/energy.
  - Automatically succeed on passive perception unless the target has mythic-level stealth.

### Stamina
- Inhuman Stamina [2]
  - +2 to Physique rolls to resist fatigue, disease, or poison.
  - Can go without sleep for up to 3 days without penalty.
- Supernatural Stamina [4]
  - +4 to Physique rolls to resist fatigue, disease, or poison.
  - Ignore all penalties from lack of sleep, thirst, or hunger for a week.
- Mythic Stamina [6]
    Immune to mundane fatigue, disease, and poison.
    Can act at full capacity for weeks without rest or food.

### Strength
- Inhuman Strength [2]
    - **Physique** checks are at +3 when involving lifting.
    - **Fight** checks are at +1 when grappling.
    - Successful attacks that depend on muscular force add 2 shifts to the result.
- Supernatural Strength [4]
    - **Physique** checks are at +6 when involving lifting.
    - **Fight** checks are at +2 when grappling.
    - Successful attacks that depend on muscular force add 4 shifts to the result.
- Mythic Strength [6]
    - **Physique** checks are at +12 when involving lifting.
    - **Fight** checks are at +3 when grappling.
    - Successful attacks that depend on muscular force add 6 shifts to the result.

### Toughness
- Inhuman Toughness [2]
    - Natural Armor:1 against all physical stress.
    - Two additional boxes of physical stress.
- Supernatural Toughness [4]
    - Natural Armor:2 against all physical stress.
    - Four additional boxes of physical stress.
- Mythic Toughness [6]
    - Natural Armor:3 against all physical stress.
    - Six additional boxes of physical stress.

## Artifical Enhancements
The above list presumes that these powers are inherent. Powers added through use of technology are different is several ways:
- Base Rules
  - Only the first tier (usually called "Inhuman") can be taken this way.
  - The Extra only costs 1 refresh instead of 2, because it comes with drawbacks.
  - The Extra requires a character aspect ("Cybernetically-Enhanced," "Never Came Off the Marine Drugs")
  - The associated aspect can be compelled to represent side effects, malfunctions, withdrawal, or
targeting of the tech by opponents
- Drawbacks
  - Maintenance or Supply Needs: Cybernetics need maintenance, and drug enhancements need a supply route
  - Vulnerabilities: Cybernetics are subject to EMP or magic interference. Biotech can be infected.
  - Recovery from a consequence involving an enhancement might require supplies that are not immediately
available.

## Creating New Powers
When a character or creature has a special ability that is not listed above, here is a guide that will work for most new
powers.

1. Define the Core Effect
  - What can the power do in the story?
  - Which skill(s) does it interact with?
2. Set Mechanical Hooks
Mix and match mechanical elements to describe how the power works.
  - Skill Bonus: Add +X to specific rolls
  - Initiative Advantage: Always act before anyone without the same tier
  - Armor Rating: Reduce incoming stress by X
  - Weapon Rating: Adds shifts of damage on a hit
  - Stress Track increase: Extra boxes on a stress track
  - Special Action: Once per scene, take a unique action without a roll or with an automatic success against certain opposition.
  - Overcome/Defend Bonuses: +X to specific overcome or defense actions.
  - Narrative Permission: Describes things you can do that break normal physics without a roll. 
3. Choose the Tier and Refresh Cost
   - Inhuman [2]
     - Skill bonus: +1 (sometimes +2 for narrow actions)
     - Weapon:2 or Armor:1 if applicable
     - Stress track increase: +2 boxes if applicable
     - Always-on passive effects are modest (run faster than normals, shrug off minor harm, sense at short ranges)
   - Supernatural [4]
     - Skill bonus: +2 (sometimes +4 for very narrow actions)
     - Weapon:4 or Armor:2 if applicable
     - Stress track +4 boxes if applicable
     - Significant passive effects (always act before normals in conflicts, ignore most mundane barriers, sense4 at long ranges)
   - Mythic [6]
     - Skill bonus: +3 (sometimes +6 for a single very narrow action)
     - Weapon:6 or Armor:3 if applicable
     - Stress track +6 boxes if applicable
     - Narrative dominance over most challenges in its domain
4. Limitations and Narrative Costs
  - Narrow Application: +2 or +4 bonuses should only apply to very specific actions to keep them balanced.
  - Trade-offs: e.g. speed powers can't be used in cramped spaces, resistance does not apply to a specific type of attack.
  - Compels: Build in situations where the GM can create trouble ("Your wings make you a big target,"
"Your speed makes stopping difficult.")
