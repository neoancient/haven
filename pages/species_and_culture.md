# Species and Culture

The **Culture** skill reflects the extent to which a character is connected with and influenced by
their culture. A character with a high **Culture** skill will have aptitudes reflecting their culture's
values. In game terms, each character selects three stunts from a list associated with their background.
These stunts allow the character to use the **Culture** skill in place of another in a particular circumstance.

Additional cultural stunts can be taken in place of standard stunts.

Players wishing to play a character with a species not on this list (troll, centaur, etc) should collaborate with
the gamemaster to develop the cultural stunts.

# Species

## Dwarf
Dwarves are stocky, bearded (including the women), and steadfast (some would say stubborn). Their traditions say they
formed from the bedrock of the mountains, and they have the fortitude and endurance to show for it. Traditionally at
home in vast cavern systems, they still prefer to make their homes underground. Known for their craftsmanship, they
also excel at engineering and mechanics. It was the Dwarves who made the first arcanomechanical star drive.

Dwarves have historically been insular and reserved in dealings with other peoples, but during the space age many of
them have integrated into human society. Humans likewise have accepted Dwarves as part of their society, and some of the
most xenophobic humans don't apply their bigotry to Dwarves.

### Dwarven Cultural Stunts

| Subsituted Skill  | Situation                                                                                      |
|:------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Athletics         | Moving in confined spaces                                                                      |
| Empathy           | Judging truthfulness, oath-keeping, or social boundaries in structured or honor-bound cultures |
| Fight             | Using an axe- or hammer-like weapon                                                            |
| Investigate       | Examining natural stone formations, underground ruins, or architectural stonework              |
| Mechanics         | Dealing with devices made of metal or stone                                                    |
| Physique          | Resisting toxins, fatigue, or intoxicants                                                      |
| Physique          | Determining extra boxes for physical stress track                                              |
| Will              | Defending against a magical mental attack                                                      |

## Elf
Elves are creatures of woodlands and mystery. Tall, lithe, and graceful, they resemble humans but have an aura about them
that makes confusion unlikely. They seem to belong to another world, or another time.

Elves have had dealings with Humans at various times, even forming close alliances on occasion. But since the beginning
of the Industrial Revolution, they have withdrawn from dealings with other peoples. As their forest dwellings came under
increased threat from pollution and encroachment, they worked with the forests to retreat from Yrth and find homes on other
worlds, requiring centuries to grow, their living ships left for the stars while Humans and Dwarves were still exploring
nearby planets. Despite gravity stones allowing rapid acceleration without harming the LifeShips or their passengers, their
inability to use astral space meant that by the time they found worlds where they could bring forth life, Humans and Dwarves
were already spreading beyond their reach and establishing outposts.

Elves have a particularly strong connection with magic. While not all Elves show direct ability to control magical
forces, their mere presence has a disrupting effect on advanced technology. This is the main reason why Elven colonies
exist within the borders of the Yrth Empire without being bothered.

### Elven Cultural Stunts

| Subsituted Skill | Situation                                                                                                                                                     |
|:-----------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Athletics        | Dodging or leaping gracefully in combat                                                                                                                       |
| Fight            | Using a sword                                                                                                                                                 |
| Lore             | When detecting magical energies                                                                                                                               |
| Mechanics        | Craft an item of art or beauty                                                                                                                                |
| Notice           | When sensing danger or subtle environmental changes                                                                                                           |
| Rapport          | Speaking to those who value art, tradition, or old ways - especially sylvan creatures                                                                         |
| Science          | When recalling events of ancient history                                                                                                                      |
| Shoot            | Using a bow                                                                                                                                                   |
| Stealth          | In a forest                                                                                                                                                   |
| Will             | Using magic to produce an enchantment, glamor, or affect growing things, even if you have no other magical ability. Each category requires a separate stunt.  |

## Gnome
Gnomes are intensely social creatures, but only with other Gnome-kind. Gnomes have few dealings with other peoples.
Gnomes found outside Gnome society are outcasts, and the separation takes a deep psychological toll on the outcast Gnome.

Gnomes are master mechanics and many of them are technomancers. Gnomes consider magic to be a branch of engineering, and
either this belief or something about Gnomes themselves allows them to use magic without disruption to technology. This
would seem to make a Gnome an ideal stardrive mechanic, but the antisocial and self-destructive behaviors displayed by
outcast Gnomes leads most ship captains to prefer dealing with the occasional tech blackout.

### Gnome Cultural Stunts

| Subsituted Skill | Situation                                                                                                                    |
|:-----------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Empathy          | Trying to detect deception or manipulation by outsiders                                                                      |
| Investigate      | Diagnosing mechanical problems, especially when the solution involves rerouting or reconfiguring                             |
| Lore             | Dealing with magical principles that can be explained with engineering logic                                                 |
| Mechanics        | Working with arcanomechanical devices                                                                                        |
| Notice           | Spot mechanical irregularities or things that don't belong in a technological setup                                          |
| Rapport          | Dealing with other Gnomes, especially to establish shared technical vocabulary or old in-jokes                               |
| Provoke          | Issuing sharp technical criticism, logic traps, or arguments based on superiority of method (It's not rude if it's correct.) |
| Will             | Maintain focus during stressful situations involving machines, magic, or time-sensitive repairs                              |
| Will             | Using magic for technomancy, even if you have no other magical ability                                                       |

## Goblin
Goblins are short (about 1m tall) humanoids with large, pointed ears and a variety of skin colors - green and purple seem
to be the most common. Dedicated tinkerers, they have their own type of genius but tend to get excited about their
projects and overlook safety precautions. Considered a hazard by most established societies, they are found mostly
in small outposts and in the Borderlands.

Goblins tend not to associate with others of their kind because they tend to have a competitive streak and fear that
other Goblins will try to steal their ideas. Then again, they tend to be the poster children for _Does Not Play Well
With Others_, as well as _Runs With Scissors_.

### Goblin Cultural Stunts

| Subsituted Skill | Situation                                                                                                                    |
|:-----------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Athletics        | Flee through chaotic environments of your own creation, such as collapsing scaffolds, exploding traps, or rolling barrels    |
| Deceive          | Confusing, misleading, or baffling someone with your reasoning or plans                                                      |
| Investigate      | Evaluating broken, half-finished, or nonsensical devices                                                                     |
| Investigate      | Scouring someone else's workshop, lab, or gear, especially if trying to reverse-engineer, repurpose, or "borrow" their ideas |
| Lore             | Identifying dangerous magical side effects, alchemical reactions, or improperly contained arcane energy                      |
| Mechanics        | Working with unreliable, experimental, or jury-rigged technology                                                             |
| Notice           | Spot gadgets, moving parts, glowing buttons, or objects clearly not designed to be pushed but still very tempting            |
| Provoke          | Escalating chaos, drawing attention, or triggering a cascade of unintended effects                                           |
| Will             | Resisting fear, humiliation, or magical effects that rely on dignity or composure                                            |

## Half-Elf
Very rare, Half-Elves result from a union between an Elf and a Human. Half-Elves have two fates before them, and must
choose to follow either the Elven path or the Human path. Half-Elven characters can select their cultural stunts from
both the Elf list and the list of their Human background. Those that choose the Elven path have the same affect on advanced
tech that Elves have, but have no restrictions on which stunts can be chosen. Those that follow the Human path do not
disrupt technology by their mere presence, but cannot select any of the Elven stunts that deal with magic.

## Half-Orc
Half-Orcs result from a union between an Orc and a Human, not usually consensual. They are usually bullied in Orc society
and not welcome in most Human society. Most Half-Orcs find themselves on the edges of civilization, trying to survive by
whatever means necessary.

Half-Orcs can select their cultural stunts from both the Orc list and a Human background list.

## Halfling
Halflings are like humans in many ways. About 1 - 1.25 meters tall (in the modern era), many Human scholars believe that
Halflings are an early offshoot of Humans - a hypothesis that Halflings almost universally reject. Despite the resemblance
to Humans, Halflings generally prefer to keep to themselves, away from the "Big People." Among Halfling virtues are Being
Respectable, Minding Your Own Business, and Hearth and Home. More traditional Halflings are suspicious of advanced
technology, but most appreciate the creature comforts of modern society.

Most Halflings can be found in the Yrth Empire, usually at the edges of Human society. Retrovert colonies tend to
be very popular with Halflings of a certain stripe.

## Orc
Orcs are warlike, revelling in raids and displays of martial prowess. Considered cruel by other peoples, Orcs believe
that the weak are flawed and deserve whatever happens to them. Ownership in Orc society is based on who is strong
enough to take what they want and keep it. Orc technology is brutally functional. They have no use for beauty. While
they rarely produce innovations of their own, they are cunning imitators, and were able to produce their star-faring
ships from a few captured samples.

The few settlements Orcs have in space are in the Borderlands, and exist primarily to provide support for
their raiding ships.

### Orc Cultural Stunts

| Empathy          | Reading members of your warband or clan                                                |

| Notice           | Spotting symbols of power, territory markings, or combat readiness                     |
| Physique         | Enduring pain or pushing past limits                                                   |
| Provoke          | Issuing a challenge, boasting, or asserting dominance                                  |
| Rapport          | Negotiating from a position of mutual respect or shared hardship                       |
| Will             | Resisting fear, initimidation, or magical influence                                    |
