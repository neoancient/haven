# The Fate System
The Pirates of Haven roleplaying system is based on the [Fate Core rules](http://www.faterpg.com). Some portions, the
magic system in particular, make use of the Dresden Files RPG, which is based on an earlier edition of the Fate system.

Fate is similar to many roleplaying games in that the characters have skills, and make dice rolls against a target
number to determine success of an action. What is distinct in Fate is Aspects. An aspect is a short descriptive phrase
that can be used, or invoked, by characters to give themselves an advantage. Aspects can also be invoked by their
adversaries. All characters have aspects. While skills tell what a character can do, aspects tell who they are. Not only
characters have aspects, though. Scenes can have aspects such as _poorly lit_, _rough terrain_, or _thin atmosphere_.
Objects can have aspects as well, and the campaign itself has aspects.

Aspects provide a narrative interface between the story and the game mechanics. They are typically triggered
through the use of fate points. A character can gain a bonus or influence the scene by spending a fate
point to invoke a relevant aspect. Aspects can also provide complications. An aspect can be compelled by the
game master to add a complication to the story, in which case the character whose aspect is compelled receives
a fate point for later use.

For more information, see  [Aspects]({{ site.baseurl }}/aspects)

# Dice and Skill Checks
The Fate system makes use of six-sided dice with two faces each marked with **+**, two with **-**, and two blank. Each
**+** is read as +1, and each **-** is read as -1. A skill check always uses four dice, leading to a result between -4
and +4. This is added to the character's skill level and compared with the target.

Skills and target numbers use the same scale, known as the Ladder:

|Number|Description|
|-----:|:----------|
|+8|Legendary|
|+7|Epic|
|+6|Fantastic|
|+5|Superb|
|+4|Great|
|+3|Good|
|+2|Fair|
|+1|Average|
|0|Mediocre|
|-1|Poor|
|-2|Terrible|

Beginning characters have skills that range from Mediocre (0) to Great (+4). A character will generally succeed more
than half the time at a task with a target number below the skill level, and very rarely at a task with a target number
four higher than the skill. Anything more difficult requires the use of stunts or invocation of one or more aspects. See
[Skills]({{ site.baseurl }}(/skills.html) for more information.

# Stunts
Stunts are special abilities that give a character an advantage in a particular situation. Other games might call them
feats or talents. A character typically begins with three stunts and can gain more with advancement. For more information,
see [Stunts]({{ site.baseurl }}/stunts.html)

# Extras
Extras are a catch-all category for character abilities that do not fit into other categories. In _Pirates of Haven_,
magical abilities are extras. Other special abilities such as superhuman strength or damage resistance are also extras,
as are items of power. In terms of character creation and advancement, an extra takes the place of a stunt. See
[Magic]({{ site.baseurl }}/magic] and (Extras)[{{ site.baseurl }}/extras)
