# Stunts

Stunts are a way of adding character-specific flavor to the way a skill is used. Stunts may reflect special training,
a particular talent, a character quirk, or any number of other things that make the character stand out from others
with the same skill. Characters start with three general stunts, in addition to three cultural stunts
(see [Species and Culture]({{ site.baseurl }}/species_and_culture.html)). A character can take additional stunts, either
general or cultural, by reducing their refresh rate by one per extra stunt.

## Defining Stunts

Like Aspects (see [Aspects]({{ site.baseurl }}/aspects.html)), stunts make a character unique. There is not a definitive
list of stunts. Though players may use any of the examples given here, they are encourage to develop custom stunts
for their characters. Players and the gamemaster should come to an agreement about how each stunt works and whether
its scope is appropriate. In general, gamemasters should not disallow any good-faith stunt, but may request a change
in its scope or limits on its use.

Stunts fall into three broad categories:

### Add a New Action to a Skill
These stunts allow you do use a skill for something that it would not normally be used for, including using
it in place of anther skill.
Examples:
- **Astral Residue Scanner:** You can use **Investigate** to detect ships that recently dropped astral in the same system.
- **Trigger Discipline:** You can use **Provoke** to inflict mental stress with a ranged weapon, assuming you don't shoot to kill.
- **Gravity Tuner:** You have worked on gravity stones, and may use **Mechanics** instead of **Arcana** when restoring, disabling, or manipulating artificial gravity systems.

### Add a Bonus to an Action
These stunts add a bonus to a skill check under a specific set of conditions.
Examples:
- **Singularity Scholar:** Because of your extensive knowledge of the Singularity Gradient Theory, you gain +2 to **Knowledge** when identifying anomalies or calculating travel times through the astral plane.
- **Spacewalker:** You have extensive experience with EV suits, and gain +2 to Athletics when when overcoming movement obstacles while in a space suit or on the outer hull of a ship.
- **Dockmaster's Friend:** You tip generously and know the right people, and gain +2 to **Contacts** when arranging berths, bribes, or quick launches at starports.

### Modify a Rule
These stunts provide an exception to the way the rules normally work in a specific situation.
Examples:
- **Legacy Hull Grease-Monkey:** You have extensive experience with outdated equipment. When working on obsolete equipment, treat any failed **Mechanics** roll as a tie (success at a cost).
- **Zero-G Acrobat:** When you succeed with style on an **Athletics** roll to dodge or move in zero-G, you may create the aspect *Perfectly Positioned* with a free invoke.

## Balancing Stunts
Stunts should be narrow enough that it feels special when they're used, but not so narrow that it rarely comes up.
Stunts can be limited by applying to one action type or a pair of action types (overcome, create aspect, attack, or defend),
or by limiting the situations in which it can be used. As a general rule, use both. Stunts that seem overpowered can be
restricted to use once per conflict, scene, or session. Stunts can also be limited by costing a fate point to invoke.
Examples:
- **Duelist's Feint:** Once per conflict you may reroll a missed melee attack against an opponent who has already attacked this turn.
- **Landing Flair:** Once per session you may avoid a consequence to the ship from a failed landing roll by taking the moderate consequence *Rattled but Alive*. 
- **Starborn Rally:** Once per session you may invoke the ancient war chant and spend a fate point to geve every ally who can hear you the boost *Fired Up* (+2 on their next action).

## Stunt Families
Specialized training or focus in a particular area of competence can be achieved through the use of stunt families. A
stunt family has a base stunt that serves as a prerequisite. Additional stunts can be added in the usual way of spending
refresh to expand the abilities of the base stunt.

### Stacking
When stunts stack, the additional stunt makes the base stunt more effective in the same situation.
- When the base stunt adds an action, add a bonus in a narrower circumstance.
- When the stunt gives a bonus to an action, give an additional +2 to the action or add an additonal two-shift effect.
- When the stunt makes a rules exception, make the exception bigger.
Examples:
- **You're Surrounded** (requires Trigger Discipline, above): When using **Provoke** to use a ranged weapon to inflict mental stress on an opponent, gain +2 when the opponent is outnumbered.
- **Who Needs a Tether?** (requires Spacewalker, above): Gain an additional +2 to Overcome Obstacle actions while in an EV suit.
- **Riposte** (requires Duelist's Feint, above): When a rerolled attack is successful, gain the *Unbalanced Opponent* boost.

### Branching
Branching effects are thematically related to the base stunt, but produce a different effect.
Example:
- **Pirate's Charisma** (base stunt): +2 to **Rapport** when dealing with criminal, fringe, or pirate contacts.
- **Silver-Tongued Devil** Once per scene, you may reroll a failed Deceive check as long as your lie is outrageous or audacious enough to seem true.
- **Brutal Reputation** +2 to **Provoke** when using fear or implied violence in negotiations.
- **Favor Called In** Once per session, you can declare that a helpful NPC owes you a favor. Describe how you earned it.
