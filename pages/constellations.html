--- layout: default title: Constellations permalink: /constellations --- Though most civilizations have found and named patterns in the sky, the Elves were the most systematic and the first to catalog the entire sky. As a result, their 88 constellations are the ones that astronomers have historically used to refer to regions of the sky. As people moved farther from Yrth, settling on worlds where many stars are in different location in the sky, a rectangular coordinate system has become the standard for scientific and technical applications. Still, the old scheme of naming objects for the region of space they appear in as seen from Yrth persists in common names of stars and is still in common use.
