      Sanronin is a terrestrial planet in the <a href="inni.html">Inni</a> system.
      The fourth planet in the Yrth system, Sanronin was the first planet outside Yrth to have a permanent settlement, but it was many years before life was possible in the open air on the surface. Replacing the carbon dioxide-rich atmosphere with one more friendly to a biosphere reduced the greenhouse effect, making it even colder. Terramancers were able to make it habitable by altering the internal geology to generate the necessary heat. Sanronin's low gravity lends itself to certain types of scientific research, low gravity sports, and retirement communities.
