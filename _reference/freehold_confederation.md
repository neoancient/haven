sections:
  - title: Overview
    content: >-
      The Freehold Confederation is a loose coalition of independent worlds, space stations, and nomadic fleets that refuse to
      acknowledge the authority of the Yrth Empire. The Confederation is built on principles of mutual defence and economic
      cooperation. Freeholders are suspicious of the Yrth Empire, those most are not openly hostile. Within the Yrth Empire,
      Freeholders are generally seen as uneducated and backwards.
  - title: Location
    content: >-
      The Freehold Confederation is located to the galactic west and slightly south of the Yrth Empire.
  - title: History
    content: >-
      The oldest worlds in the Freehold Confederation were settled by people fleeing Yrth control during the Restoration War.
      Since then it has attracted people of all sorts, from pioneers to fugitives to the curious, or anyone who does not want
      to live under the thumb of the Yrth Empire.
  - title: Government
    content: >-
      Republic, with the Council of Representatives deciding matters that impact the members as a whole, or adjudicating
      disagreements between member worlds. They have no jurisdiction over internal affairs of member worlds.
  - title: Military
    content: >-
      Individual member states maintain their own naval forces and are bound by treaty to contribute to the Freehold Militia
      for defense of other member states. Some worlds are known to use privateers.
