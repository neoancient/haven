      The New Dome is the first expansion added to <a href="!SITE_URL!/reference/haven.html">Haven</a>, added when the original dome became too crowded. Built out of
      necessity, it was simply known by its description, and even after a third dome was added it is still called
      <em>The New Dome</em>. At the time it was built, it was intended to be higher value real estate, but the more
      powerful in Haven were not interested in moving, and The Old Port (as the original dome became known) ended up as
      the more prestigious location. The New Dome became home to much of the industrial and support infrastructure.

      Besides industrial and commercial areas, there are also a number of residential areas here. Many of the people who
      work in the Old Port live in the New Dome. House ranges from nice single-unit homes to apartments to hovels.
