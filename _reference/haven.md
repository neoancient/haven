      Haven is an outpost on a rocky airless moon orbiting a gas giant in the <a href="!SITE_URL!/galaxy/rian.html">Rian</a>
      system. Initially established as a base of operations for a single crew, it has grown to include a number of
      atmospheric domes and a diverse population of pirates and outcasts.
      Haven consists of three linked atmospheric domes:
      - <a href="!SITE_URL!/reference/old_port_haven.html">The Old Port</a> is the original and largest dome.
      - <a href="!SITE_URL!/reference/new_dome_haven.html">The New Dome</a> is where most of the industrial activity is located.
      - <a href="!SITE_URL!/reference/expansion_haven.html">The Expansion</a> is the newest dome, which has yet to develop its own character.
