      The Old Port is the original and largest of <a href="!SITE_URL!/reference/haven.html">Haven</a>'s atmospheric
      domes. As Haven has expanded, those who wield most of the power are found here. It is the location of the open
      market and most of the more reputable businesses, though many who work in the Old Port cannot afford to live there.

      The fact that the powerful make their home in the Old Port in some ways makes it the safest of the three domes,
      at least in the open. Enforcers for the more powerful pirate crews are quick to respond to disruptions. While they
      are unlikely to distinguish between perpetrator and victim, people are less likely to start a public altercation.
