sections:
  - title: Appearance
    content: >-
      Emily is tall for her age and carries herself like a pirate captain-in-training. She wears a hand-stitched coat
      she “commandeered” from a drunk spacer and added custom embroidery to—stars, skulls, and an overly elaborate “E.”
      Her dark hair is pulled back with copper wire and scavenged beads, and she always wears a bandolier of tools she
      barely knows how to use. Her boots are one size too big, but she claims they belonged to a famous outlaw.
      There’s often grease on her cheek, a sparkle in her eye, and a smirk that says she’s already planning her
      next “victory.”        
  - title: Background
    content: >-
      Emily is an orphan living at Haven. She's sharp, ambitious, and determined to prove herself. Emily has decided that
      [Annie](annie.html) is her _rival_, her _mirror_, her _ultimate opponent_. Annie, meanwhile, thinks Emily is just
      kind of intense and weirdly competitive about everything from lockpicking speed to who can eat more dried squid.
      Driven, dramatic, and a bit theatrical, Emily imagines herself as a swashbuckling anti-hero, destined for
      greatness--and constantly irritated that Annie doesn't play along.
