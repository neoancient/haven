  - title: Overview
    content: >-
      The Yrth Empire is the largest and most powerful faction in the Black Ocean. It is roughly a sphere approximately
      200 light years in radius centered on Yrth.
  - title: History
    content: >-
      The Yrth Empire began on Yrth shortly after the first extrasolar colonies were established. All of Yrth was united
      under a single leader, but it was some time before the Divine Throne was sufficiently established to turn his gaze
      outward. Claiming that his divine status gave him domain over all of Yrth's colonies as well as Yrth itself, he
      began the task of bringing his scattered children, as he called them, under his protection and guidance. Some
      joined willingly, and many others joined because they lacked the resources to resist. The more independently-minded
      colonies asserted their independence and their willingness to defend it.
      
      The conflict which followed is known as the Restoration War. It lasted decades, mostly because of the distances
      involved, but individual worlds were subdued quickly and brutally. While the space around Yrth is now under the
      Empire's control, many refugees fled farther from Yrth and established themselves in the Borderlands, a region at
      the edges of the Empire's control.
  - title: Government
    content: >-
      The Yrth Empire is a theocracy ruled by an emperor known as _The Eternal Throne_, as direct reference to the emperor is taboo.
      The emperor's council is made up of priest-bureaucrats known as the Archons. Most high-ranking government officials are monastics.
      Imperial dogma says that the emperor is immortal, even though the body can age and decay. An incarnation can live centuries, but
      when the body eventually gives out it is said that the Eternal Throne is reborn. The Archons begin a search for a child born at
      same time and reveal to the empire which child is the new incarnation of the Eternal Throne.
  - title: Military
    content: >-
      The Yrth Navy is the largest and most powerful military force that has ever existed. Fleets of capital ships are capable
      of reducing cities to rubble from orbit. The ground forces are the Yrth Marines, whose abilities are enhanced through
      biological and pharmacological engineering. In addition to the formal military, law and order is enforced by patrol craft
      that handle customs enforcement and criminal investigations.
  - title: Demographics
    content: >-
      Nearly anyone and anything can be found within the Empire. Even so, the Empire is dominated by humans. Dwarves are
      also of considerable influence.
