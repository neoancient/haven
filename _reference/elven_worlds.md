title: Elven Worlds
sections:
  - title: Overview
    content: >-
      The Elves do not often interact with other peoples. They consider the humans and dwarves responsible for the ecological
      disasters that devastated their homes on Yrth, and their presence is enough to cause problems in the advanced technology
      that most other peoples depend on. While many are home among the stars, some have found worlds they could make
      habitable and settled them.
  - title: Location
    content: >-
      Astral space is very hard on the Elven Lifeships, and the few times that they have attempted faster-than-light travel
      have usually resulted in the ship contracting a wasting disease that is fatal to the ship within a few decades. As a
      result, the Elves have settled on worlds that are within the area of space controlled by the Yrth Empire, relatively
      close to Yrth. Despite the location, the Elves do not consider themselves part of the Empire, and do not welcome
      interference. The few attempts the Empire has made to exert control over Elven worlds went very badly for imperial
      forces, and the Empire has decided to ignore the presence of the Elves.
  - title: History
    content: >-
      The Elves were the first to leave Yrth and head to the stars. Sustained by their living ships, they have been able
      to travel indefinitely, as long as they visit stars frequently enough for the organic ships to replenish their
      stores of energy. Some felt the desire to have earth under their feet again. While they did not find any worlds already
      habitable, they were able to coax some worlds into erupting with life. The full number of Elven worlds is unknown, even
      by Elves themselves.
  - title: Government
    content: >-
      Each world and each Lifeship is independent.
  - title: Military
    content: >-
      No Elven naval force is known to exist.
