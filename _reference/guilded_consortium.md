  - title: Overview
    content: >-
      The Gilded Consortium is a powerful mercantile cartel that controls vast trade networks outside the Empire's
      jurisdiction.
  - title: History
    content: >-
      The Consortium was founded by a group of megacorporations who thought that any regulation by the Yrth Empire was
      too much to take and set up operations outside the Empire's reach where nothing could interfere with the goal of
      profit.
  - title: Government
    content: >-
      Corporate oligarchy. The ruling council is known as the Board of Directors.
  - title: Military
    content: >-
      The Consortium's power is based on wealth, economic manipulation, and strategic resource control rather than military
      force, though members of the Consortium maintain private security fleets to enforce their economic interests. If the
      need arises, they rely on mercenary warships to enforce contracts.
