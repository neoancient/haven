sections:
  - title: Overview
    content: >-
      The Starborn Clans are a nomadic people. They are organized into clans led by a tribal chief and senior warriors.
      Each clan has a totem creature that exemplifies the values of that clan. Within the clans, the people are organized
      into castes with warriors at the top, followed by scientists, merchants, and laborers. Combat among the clans
      (either within a clan or between clans) is heavily ritualized, with great honor derived from winning a combat with
      the smallest possible force. This often involves a bidding process in which the commanders take turns reducing the
      size of the force committed to the battle until they reach an agreement. Any disagreement in the warrior caste can
      be settled on the field of battle, with victory being proof of who is right.
  - title: Location
    content: >-
      The clans are found primarily coreward of the Yrth Empire.
  - title: History
    content: >-
      The history of the clans is shrouded in mystery. Multiple clans have different and contradictory stories about
      their origins.
  - title: Government
    content: >-
      Each clan has its own council. Membership on the council is for the most capable warriors. In theory, any warrior
      can take a seat on the council by challenging a sitting member, but in practice this is relatively rare, usually
      happening only when a warrior has aged to the point of no longer being a capable warrior. When a council member
      dies or steps down, the other members nominate a successor, and the nominees compete in battle to determine which
      takes the place.
      
      The clans as a whole also have a High Council, but it only meets once every five years unless there is some particularly
      pressing business that affects all the clans. The High Council is made up of the chieftains of the most powerful clans.
      As with the individual clan councils, a smaller clan can challenge for a seat on the High Council.
  - title: Military
    content: >-
      Each clan maintains its own military force. Individual combat is preferred, but some of the larger clans maintain some
      fleet assets.
